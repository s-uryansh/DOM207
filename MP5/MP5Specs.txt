PROJECT FOR DOM207-DOM3007-DOM6401
An Australian Government organization that was involved with this project wanted to assess the real
estate investment market in Australia and the prevailing price index there for real estate for the years 1975
through 2016. One important aspect of the investigation concerned how the per-head RE investment
behaves with respect to the index.
For this, you need to consider some sort of supply equation for the RE stock in Australia.
Although the project is not overly difficult, the question considered in the project is rather tricky.
Consider the following:
       All hypotheses should be driven by theory and logical arguments.
       Is the RE investment elasticity for price index large or small? Is it significant?
       Are the STDERRs on trend reliable in this case?
       Can you conclude that the real per-head RE investment is influenced at all by the index?
       What other factors could be captured in the trend?
       You need to check all sorts of things like data consistency, descriptive stats, validity of
        assumptions, MC, HS, interactions, quadratics, and so on and so forth.
       Dataviz is important
       Your discussions, conclusions, and recommendations must be robust (based on your findings, not
        on speculation)
